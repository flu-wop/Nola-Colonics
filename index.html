The Process

Guided, Private, Restorative.

Colon hydrotherapy is performed using a closed, sanitary system designed for your comfort, privacy, and safety. You'll lie comfortably while warm, purified water is slowly introduced through a closed system — your therapist guiding every step and offering gentle abdominal massage to encourage release.

Sessions run 35–45 minutes. Most guests leave feeling lighter, refreshed, and more relaxed.
